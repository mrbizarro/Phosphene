# Phosphene 5.0.83

Fixes:
- Choose when Phosphene updates (Free): turn Update automatically on or off in Settings (in all ten languages), or press Update and What's new whenever you are ready.
- Resume Pro downloads (Pro): interrupted Pro downloads now pick up where they left off instead of starting over.
- Lip-sync on smaller Macs (Free): real-person photos now keep mouth movement matched to the voice all the way through the finished clip.
- Long Hailuo H3 clips (Free): longer renders on slower or 32 GB Macs are no longer cut off after 20 minutes.
- Hailuo H3 References and Lip-sync (Free and Pro): your References choices are kept and checked before queueing, and Lip-sync asks before trimming audio longer than 30 seconds.
- Repair incomplete video models (Free): when a video model is missing files, Phosphene lists every missing part and offers one-click Repair to download only what is missing.
- Queued video clips (Free): quitting mid-render or an interrupted clip no longer causes false errors or stops the next clip in your queue.
- Control mode downloads (Free): when Hugging Face cannot be reached, Control mode downloads its model from a backup download source with resume.
- One Shot Resume (Free): if a previous join failed and an earlier part was removed, Resume now tells you which part is missing instead of showing an unclear join error.
- Saved voices and dialogue (Free): fixed who speaks each line across longer clips, stopped silent clips from failing, and fixed startup errors.
- Clearer error messages and Lip-sync retry (Free): failed clips explain what is known without guessing, and retrying Lip-sync starts fresh after a failed load.

Mac app: it picks up this update by itself the next time it starts. Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
