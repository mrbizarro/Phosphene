# Phosphene 5.0.66

Fixes:
- LTX videos: Phosphene now shows the LTX-2 licence once, before your first LTX download. If LTX is already on your Mac, it shows before your next LTX video. Read it, tick the box and press Accept and continue. It does not come back on this Mac. A video that is already rendering is never stopped for it.
- Report a bug and Report misuse now open an email to the Phosphene team, with your Mac's details already filled in. Before, they opened a web page that no longer loads.
- Mac app: the new Mac app (5.0.66) shows the same LTX-2 licence in first-time setup, before the video model downloads. Help > Report a Bug and the support window also open an email now.

Mac app: download the new app from phosphene.video, or let the app update itself (Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
