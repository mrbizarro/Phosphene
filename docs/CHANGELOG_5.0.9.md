# Phosphene 5.0.9

Hailuo H3 downloads inside the Mac app, with fixes for sound, music setup and updates.

## Free for everyone

- Download Hailuo H3 inside Phosphene, with progress, disk checks and Stop/Resume. If the app closes halfway through, it keeps the downloaded files.
- H3 clips play at a consistent sound level. The picture stays unchanged.
- Music setup no longer needs Apple developer tools. Resuming keeps the engine code and model files already downloaded.
- Installed Music, Speech and image engines carry over when Studio or Pro updates.
- The Mac app remembers your LTX generation choice and waits for engine installs and model downloads before updating.
- Images offers a direct route to the H3 setup it needs.
- Help → Connect an agent supplies the correct commands for your Mac installation.

## Pro fixes

- Corrected quiet sound in faster H3 clips.
- Installing Speech or changing LTX generations no longer makes the next Pro startup check reject the installation.

Phosphene stays free and open source. Pro is optional.

**Mac app:** choose Check for updates. **Pinokio:** press Update, then restart.
