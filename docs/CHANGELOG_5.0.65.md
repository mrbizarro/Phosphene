# Phosphene 5.0.65

Fixes:
- The What's new window no longer shows the same line twice.
- Storyboard: every shot is made as a quick draft first, so you can check it. Each draft now says so, with its size (for example "Draft · 640×384"), on the shot and in the player, and has a Make final button that makes that shot again at your Delivery quality. A short hint explains this the first time.
- Storyboard: a film set to High · 720p now makes its Hailuo H3 shots at High. Before, they were made at Standard.

Mac app: no new app to download; the app picks up Studio 5.0.65 on its next update check (or choose Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
