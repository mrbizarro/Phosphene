# Phosphene 5.0

**Phosphene 5.0: a native Mac app next to Pinokio, a redesigned panel, Speech and lip-sync, FLUX 3 Klein, P-H3-Image, Bench, and Pro.** Everything that was free stays free.

## Free

- **Two ways to run it.** Pinokio (press Update, then restart Phosphene) and, new, a native Mac app that needs no Pinokio and can reuse models you already downloaded: [phosphene.video/download](https://phosphene.video/download/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=release&utm_campaign=release-body&utm_content=changelog).
- **A redesigned panel.** One calmer layout, the Model picker is the first row on every surface, and the options change with the model you pick.
- **Speech and lip-sync.** Type a line, get a spoken voice, then lip-sync a face to it.
- **FLUX 3 Klein** by Black Forest Labs: pictures from text and edits with reference pictures, free (it arrived in 4.19.14 and is part of 5.0 too).
- **P-H3-Image by Pruna AI.** Big photographs, film frames and posters from a sentence, with short quoted text spelled as typed. Needs a Mac with 48 GB or more and Hailuo H3 installed.
- **Bench.** Measure what your Mac can do and compare it with other Macs.
- **Now free for everyone: Clean up sound (H3 Fast)**, which re-makes the sound of a Fast H3 clip and keeps the picture.
- **Now free for everyone: Reference picture on H3**, a picture of a character to keep in the shot.
- **Fixes carried from 4.19.x:** Colorize, Motion Control and One Shot repairs, and everything in 4.19.19.

## Pro (optional, paid)

- **Pro activation.** Paste your licence key in the app (or Pinokio) to switch Pro on.
- **Faster H3.** Picture-to-video at 5, 10 and 15 seconds renders about 1.15 to 1.23 times faster than Free on Apple Silicon with 36 GB or more, with the same faces, motion, voice and joins in our tests (measured on an M4 Max with 64 GB; other chips get a smaller gain). Text-to-video is not sped up: Pro renders it exactly as Free does.
- **H3 Flex Turbo.** A faster draft of H3 Flex, about 1.4 times quicker and a little softer. Use H3 Flex or Best for the final.

## Not in this release

- iPhone Remote is not part of 5.0; it is marked Coming soon.

In Pinokio, press Update, then restart Phosphene.
