# Phosphene 5.0.27

Free for everyone:
- Restyle a picture on Hailuo H3: in the Images screen, choose Restyle a picture, drop in a photo or drawing, and name the style in the prompt (a clay figurine, a flat vector icon, pixel art, a felt doll). You get the same scene back in that style, at about 1 megapixel, in about 2 minutes a picture (measured on an M4 Max 64 GB, 27.6 GB peak; the pre-flight says whether your Mac fits). It runs on the Hailuo H3 video pack and decodes with P-H3-Image, so both need to be installed; the chip next to Version offers the one-time 300 MB download. Bold styles work best (clay, flat vector, pixel art, felt); soft paintings are hit and miss.

Pro:
- Crisper detail on LTX-2.5 clips: a Crisper detail switch under the prompt gives noticeably finer detail (skin, hair, fabric, backgrounds) on Quick, Balanced and Standard clips, in about the same render time. Measured on an M4 Max 64 GB: about 50 to 70 % sharper fine detail, for about 10 seconds more on a 2 min 20 s clip. Off by default; the take changes when it is on. The first use downloads a 1.2 GB add-on once. Not on High or Fast draft yet, and not with a character or HDR; the Pro speed-up stands aside on a Crisper detail clip. Free renders exactly as before.

The new text is in the ten languages.

Mac app: no new app to download; the app picks up Studio 5.0.27 on its next update check (or choose Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
