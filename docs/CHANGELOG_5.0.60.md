# Phosphene 5.0.60

New, for everyone:
- Choose where your finished videos and pictures are saved, for example a folder on an external drive: Settings > Saved files > Choose folder. Each finished file is copied there and checked. Files you already made stay where they are, and the gallery still shows everything. If the drive is unplugged, new files wait on your Mac and are copied over when it is back.

Pinokio: press Update, then restart. Mac app: you get it on your next start.
