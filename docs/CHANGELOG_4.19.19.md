# Phosphene 4.19.19

**P-H3-Image by Pruna AI: big photographs, film frames and posters from a sentence, made on your Mac.** Free for everyone, on a Mac with 48 GB of memory or more that has Hailuo H3 installed.

- **P-H3-Image is in the Images tab.** Pick it in **Engine**, click the *download ~18 GB* chip once, then Generate. It is built on MiniMax H3 (the same model behind our Hailuo video) and fetched straight from Pruna, never re-hosted.
- **Words come out spelled exactly.** Put short text in double quotes and the picture carries it as typed.
- **Real sizes, not upscales.** Standard is 4 megapixels (about 2.5 minutes on an M4 Max), High is 8 megapixels (about 5 minutes), Draft is 2 megapixels (about 1.3 minutes). Each is one image straight out of the model.
- **Honest limits.** Hands and tiny background faces can still go wrong, and it does not take reference images yet.

Examples and how-to: https://phosphene.video/docs/images/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=release&utm_campaign=release-body&utm_content=changelog#p-h3-image

It uses the Hailuo H3 files you already have, so after you press Update the picker shows up once the Hailuo H3 runner has updated too (Update does this on its own).
