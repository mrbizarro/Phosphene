# Phosphene 5.0.24

Free for everyone:
- Phosphene speaks your language. The Studio now comes in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified). It follows your Mac's language by itself; to pick another, open Settings and choose Language.
- Counts and times read naturally in every language ("3 clips waiting", "5 min ago").
- A few labels that were written in pieces now read as whole sentences.
- The Pro window says what Pro does on this Mac, shows both plans with their prices, the 14-day refund and how your key arrives.

Mac app 5.0.24:
- The Mac app's own screens (welcome, setup, Pro, Support) come in the same ten languages and follow your Mac's language.
- The Mac app's Pro window says the same: what Pro does on this Mac, both plans with their prices, the refund and how your key arrives.

Pinokio: press Update, then restart. The Studio picks your Mac's language on the next start.
