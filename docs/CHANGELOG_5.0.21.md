# Phosphene 5.0.21

Free for everyone:
- Videos with LoRAs (your trained characters or styles) work again on Macs with 16 to 24 GB of memory. In 5.0.20 every such render stopped at the start with "module 'ltx_core_mlx.loader.runtime_loras' has no attribute 'declared_lora_scale'". Videos without LoRAs, and every Mac with more memory, were not affected.

Mac app: no new app to download; the app picks up Studio 5.0.21 on its next update check (or choose Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
