# Phosphene 5.0.31

Fix, free for everyone:
- Song lyrics stay honest to your idea. The lyrics writer now keeps the hard parts of a dark or angry song concept (the real details, the conflict, the anger) instead of turning them into greeting-card verse with storms, shadows and hope nobody asked for. Strong lines you wrote yourself are kept. Thanks to the report in issue #88.

Mac app: no new app to download; the app picks up Studio 5.0.31 on its next update check (or choose Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
