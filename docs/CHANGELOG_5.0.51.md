# Phosphene 5.0.51

New, for everyone:
- Settings speak plain words. The choices for how videos are made sit in one box with their own Save and Undo; everything else saves the moment you change it.
- If Phosphene has not finished setting up, a calm card under the top bar says so in one sentence, with one Finish setup button, instead of a red warning. In all ten languages.

Mac app: you get it on your next start. Pinokio: press Update to get it.
