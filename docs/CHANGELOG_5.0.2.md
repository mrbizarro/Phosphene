# Phosphene 5.0.2

**Pro that stays on, Remote that starts, and a clearer way back when something goes wrong.** Nothing that was free was taken away.

## Pro (optional, paid) in the Pinokio install

- **Pro stays on.** An unreadable saved licence is never treated as "no licence". Settings > Pro always shows your saved licence, also while Pro is off, and a paid renewal switches Pro back on by itself, also after a restart.
- **One Mac, both installs.** The Mac app and Pinokio on the same Mac share one place on your key. Activating in one no longer switches off the other. "Deactivate this Mac" frees the whole Mac.
- **Honest activation.** If the pass cannot be saved, activation is not reported as done, the key you typed stays in the box, and the server slot is given back.
- **No late surprises.** A slow check that finishes after you deactivated or changed key can no longer turn Pro back on.
- **Settings > Pro keeps the key box** when Pro comes from the Mac app's pass, so you can activate this install on its own.

## iPhone Remote

- **Remote setup shows what it is doing:** downloading, checking, a clear error with Try again, and "restart Phosphene" when new parts need it.
- **Pro parts update safely.** The previous working part is kept and restored if the new one fails to import or start.

## Editor

- **The Editor opens on an empty timeline** you can drag clips into, with no start screen in the way. The header says "not saved yet" until a clip lands, then "saved". Nothing is written to disk before that.

## Everywhere in the app

- **One purple Pro tag** beside the wordmark, wherever Pro is shown. The Support button is easier to find.
- **Volume in the play bar.** A speaker and slider sit next to the time in the viewer, also full screen. Press `M` to mute.
- **Reuse from Pinokio.** If Pinokio already holds a model or adapter, Phosphene offers to use it first: it is cloned on your disk, so there is no download and no extra space. Settings > Models has a "Reuse from Pinokio" button on each model, and Upscale & Face Fix says "Use it from your Pinokio install" when its adapter is missing.
- **Set up Remote** is a step you can follow: it shows the pairing code or QR once Remote is on.
- **Free users see what Pro adds:** a small "Pro" mark on Remote Control, and Faster Flex Turbo is listed as a Pro option with a line saying what it does.

## Free

- Nothing was taken away from Free. The Editor start, the volume control and Reuse from Pinokio are for everyone.
