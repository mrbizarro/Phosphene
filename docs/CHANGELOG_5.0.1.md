# Phosphene 5.0.1

**Pro now works on Pinokio.** Paste your licence key and the perks switch on. Everything that was free stays free.

## Pro (optional, paid)

- **Pinokio takes your Pro licence.** Open Pro in the header, paste your licence key, and Pro turns on for this Mac. No Mac app needed. If the Mac app is already on your Mac with Pro, Pinokio uses it automatically. One Mac per licence; Phosphene checks once a day while you are online and keeps working for two weeks without internet.
- **Faster Flex Turbo.** A faster way to render Hailuo H3 Flex, on as soon as your licence is.
- **iPhone Remote.** Start and watch renders from the free Phosphene Remote app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6827562294)), at home or away. Works with Pinokio and the Mac app.

## Free

- Nothing changes for Free. Without a licence Phosphene behaves exactly as 5.0.
