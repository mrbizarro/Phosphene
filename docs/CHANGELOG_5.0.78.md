# Phosphene 5.0.78

New for everyone (Free):
- Characters with voices, Free. Voices (in the left column) saves a voice from 10 to 60 seconds of one person talking: a video, an audio file or the microphone. Phosphene cleans the sound and learns the voice by itself. New character takes a name, up to 3 pictures and a saved voice. On Hailuo H3, start a line with the character's name, for example "Aria: Where were you last night?", and that line is spoken in their voice after the render, so two characters can talk to each other in one scene. Learning a voice needs 24 GB of memory and, the first time, about 29 GB of downloads.
- Delete, Free: delete a video or picture from your Outputs. It goes to the Mac's Trash, and Undo brings it back for 15 minutes while Phosphene is open.
- Write it for me on Hailuo H3, Free: type a short idea and a prompt writer on your Mac turns it into a full prompt, keeping any spoken line word for word. You read it before it is used. The first time, it sets itself up once (an 18.8 GB download).
- Upscale & Face Fix on smaller Macs, Free: it takes more clips, and before you press Generate it tells you if a clip is too long for this Mac and how much to trim.
- In mainland China, setting up Phosphene's tools now has backup download sources when the usual ones are blocked, and Report a bug can open your mail app with the report.

New in Pro:
- Characters in Hailuo H3 References, Pro: give a character up to 3 pictures and their saved voice, and they keep their look and speak in their own voice.
- Re-voice, Pro: change who says a line in a finished video or audio file. Phosphene finds every line; pick a saved voice for the ones you want to change. The picture and the rest of the sound stay as they are, and a new copy is saved next to the original. Find it under More on any clip.

The new text is in the ten languages.

Mac app: it picks up this update by itself the next time it starts. Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
