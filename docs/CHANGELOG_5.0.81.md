# Phosphene 5.0.81

Released 2026-11-22. Bug-fix release for the Studio and the Mac app.

## Fixed
- **Choose when Phosphene updates (Mac app, Free):** Turn automatic updates off in Settings when you want to decide when Phosphene updates. Your previous version keeps working until a new update finishes setting up.
- **Keep models working after moving to an external drive (Mac app, Free):** Moving models to an external drive keeps them working. Opening the updated Mac app also repairs links broken by an earlier move, without downloading models again.
- **Mac app setup in your language and backup download for China (Mac app, Free):** Setup messages now follow your chosen language, with a verified backup source in China if the main download cannot connect.
- **Clearer repair message for damaged Hailuo H3 files (Studio, Free):** When a Hailuo H3 file on your Mac is damaged, the queue card now points to Repair instead of asking you to Retry.

Pinokio: press Update, then restart. Mac app: open Phosphene to update, or download the new Mac app at [phosphene.video](https://phosphene.video/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=release&utm_campaign=release-body).
