# Phosphene 5.0.87

Fixes (Free):
- Song covers: a cover no longer fails when a few notes of the original song are hard to read. Phosphene rebuilds the melody from what it heard and carries on.
- Pictures: if a reference picture you added has been deleted, Phosphene tells you before the job joins the queue, and deleting a picture also removes it from the picture slots.
- Music: songs no longer stop with a memory warning while your Mac still has plenty of free memory.
- Hailuo H3: when macOS stops a render, the message now says whether the Mac ran out of graphics time or the graphics driver failed, and what to try.
- Mac app: Train Character downloads its models again after the app has updated itself.

Mac app: it picks up this update by itself the next time it starts. Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
