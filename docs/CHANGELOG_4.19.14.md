# Phosphene 4.19.14

- New in Images: **FLUX 3 Klein by Black Forest Labs.** Make an image from a sentence, or add reference pictures and describe the change you want.
- After the first run, each image takes about 50 seconds on an M4 Max with 64 GB. Choose **Animate** on any image to turn it into a video.
- Your first image sets up FLUX 3: about 1 GB for the engine plus the model (7.6 GB at Q6, 9.9 GB at Q8, 5.3 GB at Q4). Stop cancels setup; the next run picks up where it left off.
- FLUX 3 asks you to accept its licence once, before anything downloads. Commercial use of the images is allowed below USD 5 million in yearly revenue; read the terms on first use.
- Needs 32 GB of memory or more. A 24 GB Mac can use Q4 at a small size with one reference.

In Pinokio, press Update, then restart Phosphene. Nothing downloads until you choose FLUX 3.
