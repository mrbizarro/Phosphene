# Phosphene 5.0.25

Free for everyone:
- Long LTX clips use a third less memory at the end of the render. On Macs with more than 32 GB, an LTX clip longer than 5 seconds now finishes in 128-frame pieces instead of one. Measured on 10-second clips: the memory peak at the end drops from 20 GB to 12.7 GB at 1024x576 and from 30 GB to 19 GB at 720p, in the same time and with no visible difference. Macs with 32 GB or less already rendered in pieces and are unchanged.

Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
