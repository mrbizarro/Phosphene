# Phosphene 5.0.62

Fixes, Pro:
- References on Hailuo H3: after its download, References sets itself up once on your Mac. Your Hailuo H3 clips now wait in the queue while that happens, with a line that says so, instead of both running at once. Other clips keep going. If a Hailuo H3 clip is already rendering, the setup waits for it to finish.
- A picture References cannot use is now explained before the render starts, not minutes in: Picture 1 left empty while Picture 2 or 3 is filled, or a picture more than 4 times wider than it is tall (or taller than it is wide).
- If References needs a newer Hailuo H3, it now says how to get it instead of showing the Pro offer.

Mac app: no new app to download; the app picks up Studio 5.0.62 on its next update check (or choose Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
