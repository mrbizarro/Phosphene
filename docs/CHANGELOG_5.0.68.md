# Phosphene 5.0.68

Fixes:
- Lip-sync: on the Mac app, the mouth could run a little ahead of the voice and the player showed no lip-sync score. After each Lip-sync video, Phosphene now lines the mouth up with the voice again and shows the score in the player.
- Continue the song finds the face again on the Mac app, instead of saying it could not find one.
- The first Lip-sync video after this update adds a small face-finding tool by itself, once. Nothing else on your Mac changes.

Mac app: it picks up this update by itself the next time it starts. Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
