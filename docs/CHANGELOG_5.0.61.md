Pro:
- References on Hailuo H3: build a new shot around up to 3 pictures of people, things or places, and they keep their look in the scene your prompt describes (the prompt can name them, for example "the woman from Picture 2"). On Text, switch on References under the first picture slot; that picture is Picture 1 and two more slots open. Clips are 5 seconds. It uses MiniMax's own reference model: a one-time download of about 43 GB, which the app explains before it starts, shows progress for and resumes after a stop. Macs with 32 GB of memory or more. Expected in Free around January 2027, not guaranteed.

The new text is in the ten languages.

Mac app: no new app to download; the app picks up Studio 5.0.61 on its next update check (or choose Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
