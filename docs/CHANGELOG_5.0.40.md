# Phosphene 5.0.40

Fix, free for everyone:
- Audio > Music Video: you can drag a song into the song slot, or click it to pick one, again. After the Image Studio had been opened, the song slot took only pictures, so a song could not be dropped in or chosen.

Mac app: no new app to download; the app picks up Studio 5.0.40 on its next update check (or choose Check for Updates). Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
