# Phosphene 5.0.49

Fixes, for everyone:
- On a busy Mac, a render that takes more than two minutes to get going now waits a few minutes longer instead of failing and telling you to reinstall.
- If your video model's folder goes missing while clips wait (a drive unplugged, a folder moved), the clip now says to put it back or download the model again, instead of a confusing error.

Mac app: you get it on your next start. Pinokio: press Update to get it.
