# Phosphene 5.0.53

Fixes, for Macs with 16 GB of memory:
- Hailuo H3 now offers clips up to 5 s on these Macs. The 10 s and 15 s choices are greyed out with a short reason, so a long clip no longer stops halfway with a memory error. Macs with more memory keep every length.
- If a Hailuo H3 clip runs out of memory, Phosphene now says what to try: a 5 s clip, a lower setting such as Draft, and quitting other apps.

Mac app: you get it on your next start. Pinokio: press Update to get it.
