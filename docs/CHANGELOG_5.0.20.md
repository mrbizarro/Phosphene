# Phosphene 5.0.20

Free for everyone:
- Less memory on 16 GB Macs: an LTX-2.5 video peaks at about 5.7 GB instead of 9 GB, and a render with LoRAs at about 6.7 GB instead of 19 GB. Same frames as before.
- Mac app 5.0.20: keep your models on an external drive. Choose where models live at setup or in Settings, and move them there or back later. An unplugged drive shows a "drive isn't connected" screen instead of an error.
- More new installs finish their first video: if the Mac app's model download stopped, the Studio fetches the video model itself; the first clip is sized to fit the Mac; a "Your first video is ready" moment; no What's new pop-up before the first video.
- Mac app 5.0.20: "Set up the studio first" is gone. Background downloads use the Studio that setup installed, an unfinished setup shows a Finish setup button, and a failed download no longer hides the Studio's own card.
- On a small Mac, a MiniMax H3 render that cannot get memory waits its turn instead of failing every job behind it.
- A cleaner What's new window: headline, New for everyone, New in Pro, and how-to clips at their real size.
- After the third finished render, Phosphene asks once for an App Store review (with the iPhone app paired) or a GitHub star.
- The README is rewritten, and the iPhone app is described as part of Pro.

Pro:
- Faster LTX clips now reach 16 to 24 GB Macs: about 27 to 30% faster than Free.

Mac app: choose Check for Updates; this installs the Mac app 5.0.20 with Studio and Pro 5.0.20. Pinokio: press Update, then restart.
