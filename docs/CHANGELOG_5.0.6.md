# Phosphene 5.0.6

**Your Pro licence stays on your Mac when you delete and reinstall Phosphene in Pinokio.** Nothing that was free was taken away.

## Pro

- **No more pasting your key again after a reinstall.** On Pinokio, Phosphene used to keep the Pro licence inside the app's own folder, so deleting the app in Pinokio and installing it again lost it. The licence is now kept in your Mac's Keychain, outside the app, so a reinstall picks it up by itself and Pro is on as soon as the app opens. If your Mac's Keychain cannot be used, it is kept in a private file in your Phosphene folder instead.
- **Nothing to do if you already have Pro.** The first time 5.0.6 starts, it copies your existing licence to the new place.
- **Lost your key?** Under the key box: **Already paid? Restore Pro**. We email your key again, to the address you bought it with.
- The licence check itself has not changed: one Mac per licence, a daily check while you are online, two weeks without internet.

## Everywhere

- Press **Update**, then restart (Pinokio).

## Free

- Nothing was taken away from Free. Pro customers only notice that the key is not asked for again.
